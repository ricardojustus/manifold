# Mission Contract — template (Part 4)

One agreement, shaped WITH the operator at the start, that makes their absence safe until DELIVER. The five
invariants below appear in every contract; everything else — sections, length, phases, format —
is yours to shape per the work. A sixth standing invariant is not added without their ruling.

**Contract-lite** (small work) — one line in chat — still carries all five: the goal and what done
means (1), the recorded defaults by reference (2), the standard exits (3), the budget word (4),
receipts at delivery (5). The line states 1 and 4 and the done-check; the rest bind by default.

---

## <Mission name> — contract (DD/MM)

**Vision / goal** — what the operator wants and why, in their terms. **Shape by interview, never by guess**:
one question at a time, offer 2-3 genuinely different approaches, converge — a one-shotted vision
guess costs dozens of corrective revisions.

**1. Definition of done**
- Machine-checkable wherever possible. Preferred form: the native session **goal condition**
  (≤4,000 chars; evaluated by a SEPARATE evaluator, one-keypress approval — CC ≥2.1.233).
  Otherwise: named tests, checks, comparisons an evaluator outside this session runs.
- Taste bars where judgment rules (visual/UX): both-ends loop + variant artifacts; any visual
  deliverable's done includes "the agent looked at it".
- **The done-check never runs in the acting session.** Deterministic/held-out checks are the
  gate; LLM judgment is advisory; receipts bind to content (diff SHAs, test output), never to a
  file existing.

**2. Granted boundaries** — what runs without the operator THIS arc. Defaults come from the
overlay's recorded contract defaults (see the project walls). Where none are recorded: merges need
the operator's word; promotion and external writes are not granted. The contract may narrow or
widen; the operator's accept binds only what this section says it means. **The arc's REVIEW PLAN is written here at
SHAPE**: the ladder rung, the stopping rule, and a hard cap — mid-arc sessions execute the plan,
they never re-decide it.

**3. Exit conditions** — the run ends ONLY by a written rule: done · budget wall · floor wall ·
blocked-on-world (something only the operator holds). Non-convergence (a verifier loop whose findings are
flat or growing after ~3 rounds) is an exit, not a retry budget.

**4. Budget** — the word (trivial / noticeable / heavy) + the envelope, semantics per floor #5,
priced here once. Metered dollars always get the arithmetic.

**5. Receipts at DELIVER** — what shipped · done-state per DoD item with the evaluator's evidence
· deviations · spend vs envelope · the plain-language explainer · open questions parked. Floor
#7 governs every line. During RUN: digest per sitting + live status artifact; nothing else
reaches the operator unless they look in.
